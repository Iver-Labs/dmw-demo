## Your Job Search

There is no substitute for doing it right the first time! Your search for a job overseas must begin in the right, legal way. This means dealing only with duly authorized recruitment agencies.

The Philippine Overseas Employment Administration (POEA) issues licenses to these agencies to recruit and employ Filipino overseas. Their participation in the country's overseas employment program is crucial: they take charge of the recruitment, hiring, and deployment of our countrymen as well as ensure the strict implementation of employment contracts.

The POEA does not encourage direct hiring or name hiring as employers have no local representatives to answer for any untoward incident.

## The Recruitment Agency

To make sure you're dealing with a duly licensed recruitment agency, simply log on to www.poea.gov.ph. This is the POEA official website. Check the recruitment agency's name in the list of licensed companies. Make sure it also has an approved job order for the position you are applying for.

A company not included in the POEA list is an illegal recruiter. DO NOT DEAL WITH IT!

## Documents when applying through a recruitment agency

When applying through a recruitment agency, have the following documents ready:

1. Birth Certificate issued by the National Statistics Office (NSO). For married women, obtain an NSO copy of your marriage contract
2. Muslim applicants need a certification from the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos
3. Transcript of School Records and Diploma authenticated by the Department of Education or Commission on Higher Education
4. Training Certificates from TESDA or training institute or company
5. These documents will be processed by the agency and submitted to the employer.

## When the employer selects you

When you receive the good news that the employer has selected you, proceed to do the following:

1. Read and study carefully the Employment Contract presented to you. If you agree to all the provisions, sign it.
2. Undergo medical examination in a DOH-accredited clinic; submit the result to agency.
3. Attend the Pre-Departure Orientation Seminar (PDOS) in an OWWA-accredited PDOS provider and get your Certificate of Attendance; submit this to the agency.

Note: It is at this point that the agency will submit all your documents to the POEA for processing. The POEA will then issue an e-Receipt also known as the Overseas Employment Certificate (OEC).

## Government Placement Branch (GPB)

You may also check out POEA's Government Placement Branch (GPB). Governments of other countries deal directly with the POEA to recruit workers from the Philippines. These countries have their respective recruitment programs. For example:

- South Korea - Employment Permit System
- Japan - PJEPA
- Germany - Triple Win

The GPB also recruits nurses and other medical health workers for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia-Ministry of Health.

Applicants for the GPB job openings must register online through the POEA's website and the Manpower Registry Division.

## Government Placement Branch (GPB): documents

The following documents must also be ready:

1. NSO issued Birth Certificate
2. NSO issued marriage contract
3. National Commission on Muslim Filipinos certification for Muslims
4. DepEd or CHED Transcript of Records or Diploma
5. TESDA or training center certificate
6. Two (2) pieces passport size or 2"x2" pictures
7. Six (6) pieces passport size or 2"x2" pictures for Ministry of Health-KSA applicants
8. Certificates of Employment of at least two (2) years' experience in one position
9. Passport (for applicants with overseas job experience)

If applicable: License/Board; valid Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) Identification Card; Board License; Board Rating; Post graduate diploma.

Thereafter, have your medical examination in a DOH accredited clinic as well as do the following: attend the Pre-Departure Orientation Seminar (PDOS) conducted by OWWA at the POEA building and get your Certificate of Attendance; submit the medical result and PDOS Certificate of Attendance to GPB. Obtain your e-Receipt/Overseas Employment Certificate (OEC).

## Name/Direct Hiring

Browsing through the web, periodicals, and/or referrals from relatives and friends may give you a job lead overseas.

This is what is commonly referred to as NAME-HIRING or DIRECT HIRING. A Name-Hire applicant is one who seeks employment on his own without the participation of a third party, or a recruitment agency. Tread cautiously!

Great care must be exercised when considering this option. Applicants are strongly advised to coordinate directly with the POEA's Name Hire Processing Unit. A personal visit to the Unit will be greatly advantageous to you.

Your prospective employer will prepare an Employment Contract in your name and submit it to the nearest Philippine embassy for authentication and verification. The employer will take charge of your visa and work permit in the host country, send you a round-trip airline ticket, and money for processing fees.

## Name/Direct Hiring: documents and steps

Prepare the following documents:

1. NSO issued Birth Certificate
2. NSO issued marriage contract
3. National Commission on Muslim Filipinos certification for Muslims
4. DepEd or CHED Transcript of Records or Diploma
5. TESDA or training center certificate

You need to do the following, too:

1. Undergo your medical examination at an accredited DOH clinic, if required; obtain a copy of your Medical Certificate.
2. Attend PDOS conducted by OWWA at the POEA and obtain the Certificate of Attendance;
3. Submit your Employment Contract/Offer of Employment, employment visa/work permit, together with the medical and PDOS certificates at the Name Hire Processing Center, 2nd Floor, Blas F. Ople Building, Ortigas Ave., cor EDSA, Mandaluyong City.

## The Overseas Employment Certificate (OEC)

Here's a very important footnote about the Overseas Employment Certificate (OEC).

1. The OEC is proof that your documents have been processed at the POEA, including payments for POEA processing fee, OWWA, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions.
2. Remember, only documented workers are entitled to the full protection of the law as well as the benefits of the programs and services of the government. Your OEC has a 2-month validity from the date of issuance; this means the agency should deploy you within this period.
3. Your OEC is your travel exit clearance at the airport when you leave. You are exempted from paying the travel tax and terminal fee!
